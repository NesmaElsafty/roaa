Services / products

The work between the drawing and the handover

Eight services, taken from how ROAA describes what it does. Each one can stand alone or sit inside a turn-key project.

01

Complete turn-key finishing

Units finished from the shell through to handover, so the client receives a space that is ready to use.

02

Interior design and build

Personal, customized interiors. Design and construction stay with one team, so the drawing and the room match.

03

Space planning

Planning for offices, homes, and commercial rooms before finishes are chosen.

04

Kitchens and dressings

Design and execution of kitchens and dressing rooms, including the joinery that makes them work.

05

Lighting and security

Lighting design together with security systems, including surveillance where the project requires it.

06

Landscape and swimming pools

Gardens, hardscape, and pools, designed and executed as part of the grounds.

07

Custom furniture

Furniture designed and manufactured for corporate, commercial, and residential spaces.

08

Wood products and cladding

Wooden products manufactured in-house, and wood cladding installed on site.
