Who we are?

ROAA is a Cairo-based construction and finishing company that was founded since 1998 under the name of Amoun and in 2005 it turned to be under the acquisition of ROAA Construction and Finishing - Sherif Shawky El Gohary. The company is specialized in distinctive commercial and residential projects.

What we do?

  • Complete Turn Key Finishing for Units
  • Personal, Customized Interior Design and Build
  • Innovative Space Planning
  • Kitchens & Dressings Design and Execution
  • Lighting and security systems
  • Landscape and Swimming Pools Design and Execution
  • Customized Furniture Design and Manufacture for Corporate, Commercial and Residential Spaces
  • Manufacturing Wooden Products and Execute wood Cladding Work

Our Mission

To work closely with our clients on meeting the rising demand for creating spaces that beyond original expectation, deliver integrated construction and finishing services while maintaining the highest quality and ontime delivery. Our aim is to offer simplified service that is direct, holistic and most importantly hassle free.

Our Vision

ROAA aspires to become a leading company in Egypt in the field of construction and finishing with a workforce that can achieve extraordinary results, in addition to offering the highest quality standards in parallel with serving the customer service by providing company's core values - HSE, Quality, Reliability, Innovation, Integrity and Growth.

Our Area of Expertise

Building sustainable projects from concept to completion is our area of expertise for the following sectors: Corporate, Hospitality, Commercial and Residential.

Corporate: Covering head offices and banking industry.

Hospitality: Hotels and Resorts.

Residential: Villas, Appartments and Turn Key Projects.

Commercial: Stores, Restaurants, Cafes and Centres

ISO Certification

ISO 9001:2015 for construction and finishing works. Certificate No. UKS-QMS-25-107, registered 29.07.2025 and valid until 28.07.2026.
